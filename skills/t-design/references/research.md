# Research guide

Use the web search and fetch tools your harness has. If there are none, say so and mark what you could not check as [A].

## Sources

- Open every source you cite, in this session, and copy figures from the page. Never cite from memory: in tests, 18 to 55% of chatbot citations were fabricated, and AI search tools got over 60% of citation queries wrong.
- Trace each claim to its original. Stop, check who the source is, look for better coverage, and follow quotes back to where they started (SIFT).
- Grade each source in the Sources table: Measured, Documented practice, Anecdote or Marketing. Vendor pages and analyst press releases are Marketing unless the method is public.

## What to research

- Alternatives: products, spreadsheets, paper and doing nothing. Use each product's own site and pricing page.
- Evidence against: why similar products failed, and what their users complain about. Record the searches even when they find nothing.
- Rules: name each trigger (personal data, payments, health, children, location) and link the official text. Draw no legal conclusions.
- Feasibility: official docs, limits, pricing and SDK status for the riskiest technical parts. A quick throwaway probe beats an opinion, when it needs no credentials.
- Market size: leave it out unless the human asks. If asked, build it bottom-up from sourced counts and prices, show the formula and label it [E].

## Quick

6 to 12 sources: 3 or more alternatives, official docs for the one or two riskiest technical parts, and a check for rule triggers.

## Deep

15 to 30 sources: Quick's kinds, plus 5 to 8 alternatives with independent reviews or complaints, places where target users describe the problem (Anecdote), the primary text of each rule that applies, a search for evidence against, API limits and pricing, and one throwaway probe when no credentials are needed. Also write a five-question script, in the style of the story questions, for the human to interview 3 to 5 real users.

The counts and times are working defaults, not measured values.
